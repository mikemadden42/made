# system settings

The **System Settings** app was introduced in **macOS Ventura** (version 13), which was announced at WWDC on **June 6, 2022**, and released on **October 24, 2022**. It replaced the older **System Preferences** app with a redesigned interface, making it more consistent with the Settings app found on iOS and iPadOS. The new design focuses on a sidebar layout, which allows for easier navigation across various settings.

The steps below cover macOS **Ventura (13)**, **Sonoma (14)**, **Sequoia (15)**, and **Tahoe (26)**. Where a setting moved between releases, the version-specific path is noted. If you can't find a pane, type its name into the search field at the top of the System Settings sidebar.

---

1. **Select ‘Dark’ Appearance**:
   - Go to **System Settings** > **Appearance** and select **Dark**.

2. **Select 'Blue' Accent color**:
   - Go to **System Settings** > **Appearance** > **Accent color** and select **Blue**.

3. **Set Google Chrome as ‘Default web browser’**:
   - Go to **System Settings** > **Desktop & Dock** > Scroll down to **Default web browser** and choose **Google Chrome**.

4. **Set ‘Screen Saver’ to Message**:
   - Go to **System Settings** > **Screen Saver** and select **Message** (under **Other** on Sonoma and later). Click **Options** to set the message text.

5. **Set ‘Start after’ to 5 minutes**:
   - Go to **System Settings** > **Lock Screen** > **Start Screen Saver when inactive** and set it to **For 5 minutes**.

6. **Show a clock on the Screen Saver**:
   - On **Ventura (13)**: Go to **System Settings** > **Screen Saver** and enable **Show with clock**.
   - On **Sonoma (14)** and later: **Show with clock** was removed. Go to **System Settings** > **Lock Screen** > **Show large clock** and select **On Screen Saver and Lock Screen**.

7. **Set lower right Hot Corner to ‘Start Screen Saver’**:
   - Go to **System Settings** > **Desktop & Dock** > Scroll down and click **Hot Corners…**, then set the **Lower Right** corner to **Start Screen Saver**.

8. **Add more Spaces**:
   - Invoke **Mission Control** (press **F3**, **Control + Up Arrow**, or swipe up with three or four fingers). Hover near the top of the screen and click the **+** that appears in the upper-right corner to add a Space.

9. **Set ‘Computer Name’**:
   - Go to **System Settings** > **General** > **About** and edit the **Name** field. (The Local Hostname can be edited at the bottom of **General** > **Sharing**.)

10. **Customize the menu bar clock**:
    - On **Ventura (13)** through **Sequoia (15)**: Go to **System Settings** > **Control Center** > **Clock Options…**.
    - On **Tahoe (26)**: Go to **System Settings** > **Menu Bar** > **Clock Options…**.
    - Adjust the date, day of week, and seconds display as desired. The 24-hour format itself is controlled by the next step.

11. **Turn on ‘24-hour time’** (system-wide time format):
    - Go to **System Settings** > **General** > **Date & Time** and enable **24-hour time**. (The same setting appears as **24-Hour Time** under **General** > **Language & Region**; the two are linked.)

12. **Set ‘Login window shows: List of users’**:
    - Go to **System Settings** > **Lock Screen** > **Login window shows** and select **List of users**.

13. **Configure Dock settings**:
    - Go to **System Settings** > **Desktop & Dock** and adjust the following:
      - **Position on screen**: Set to **Left**.
      - **Automatically hide and show the Dock**: Enable.
      - **Magnification**: Enable and adjust the slider.

14. **Set 'Require password' immediately**:
    - Go to **System Settings** > **Lock Screen** and set **Require password after screen saver begins or display is turned off** to **Immediately**.

15. **Enable FileVault**:
    - Go to **System Settings** > **Privacy & Security** > Scroll down to **FileVault** and turn it on, saving the recovery key as prompted.

16. **Turn on Firewall**:
    - Go to **System Settings** > **Network** > **Firewall** and turn it on.

17. **Enable Full Disk Access for Terminal & iTerm2**:
    - Go to **System Settings** > **Privacy & Security** > **Full Disk Access** and turn on **Terminal** and **iTerm2** (use **+** to add an app that isn't listed).

18. **Schedule ‘Do Not Disturb’ for quiet hours**:
    - Go to **System Settings** > **Focus** > **Do Not Disturb** > **Add Schedule…** and choose **Time** to define an automatic on/off window (for example, overnight). The legacy "when the display is sleeping" trigger was removed when Focus replaced the original Do Not Disturb pane in macOS 12 Monterey.

19. **Restrict Remote Login to specific users**:
    - Go to **System Settings** > **General** > **Sharing**, click the **ⓘ** next to **Remote Login**, set **Allow access for:** to **Only these users:**, and add the local user.

20. **Set iCloud preferences**:
    - On **Ventura (13)** and **Sonoma (14)**: Go to **System Settings** > **Apple ID** (your name at the top of the sidebar) > **iCloud**.
    - On **Sequoia (15)** and later: Go to **System Settings** > **Apple Account** (your name at the top of the sidebar) > **iCloud**.

21. **Lock screen shortcuts**:
    - **Control + Command + Q** locks the screen immediately.
    - **Control + Shift + Power** (or **Media Eject**) puts the displays to sleep, which locks the screen when a password is required immediately (step 14).

22. **Set Night Shift Schedule**:
    - Go to **System Settings** > **Displays** > **Night Shift…** and set **Schedule** to **Sunset to Sunrise**.

23. **Set alert style to Banners for apps**:
    - Go to **System Settings** > **Notifications** > Select an app and choose **Banners** as the alert style.

24. **Show Bluetooth in the menu bar**:
    - On **Ventura (13)** through **Sequoia (15)**: Go to **System Settings** > **Control Center** > **Bluetooth** and choose **Show in Menu Bar**.
    - On **Tahoe (26)**: Go to **System Settings** > **Menu Bar** and turn on **Bluetooth** under **Menu Bar Controls**.

25. **Enable Automatic Graphics Switching** (Intel MacBook Pro with discrete GPU only):
    - Go to **System Settings** > **Battery** > **Options…** and enable **Automatic graphics switching**. This option does not exist on Apple silicon Macs.

26. **Disable Siri**:
    - On **Ventura (13)** and **Sonoma (14)**: Go to **System Settings** > **Siri & Spotlight** and turn off **Ask Siri**.
    - On **Sequoia (15)** and later: Go to **System Settings** > **Apple Intelligence & Siri** (or **Siri** on Macs without Apple Intelligence) and turn off **Siri**.

27. **Disable Screen Time**:
    - Go to **System Settings** > **Screen Time** > **App & Website Activity** and click **Turn Off App & Website Activity**.

28. **Enable Touch ID**:
    - Go to **System Settings** > **Touch ID & Password** and click **Add Fingerprint**.

29. **Add a printer**:
    - Go to **System Settings** > **Printers & Scanners** > **Add Printer, Scanner, or Fax…**.

30. **Configure Time Machine**:
    - Go to **System Settings** > **General** > **Time Machine**. Use **Options…** to exclude drives or folders and to change the backup frequency (choose **Manually** to stop automatic backups), or select the backup disk and click **−** to remove it.

---

Apple's Mac User Guide for the current release is at <https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/welcome/mac> and can confirm any path that shifts in a future update.
